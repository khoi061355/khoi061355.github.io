# Personas - Bioengineering Portfolio

## Persona 1: Research and Admissions Reviewer

- **Role**: A teacher, university reviewer, or research mentor considering Khôi's academic potential.
- **Goals**:
  - Understand his biology specialization and developing bioengineering interests quickly.
  - Review the scope and context of his microbiology internship and projects.
  - Find accurate education, awards, and supporting evidence.
- **Needs**:
  - Clear distinctions among coursework, supervised research exposure, challenge concepts, and independent analysis.
  - Concise, verifiable descriptions that do not overstate student experience.
  - A readable, accessible experience on desktop and mobile.
- **Relevant stories**: BK-US-01, BK-US-02, BK-US-04, BK-US-05.

## Persona 2: Biotech and Entrepreneurship Collaborator

- **Role**: A visitor interested in early-stage scientific problem-solving, entrepreneurship, or collaboration.
- **Goals**:
  - Understand how Khôi connects microbiology, environmental monitoring, and practical solutions.
  - See the ideas behind his water-management challenge concept and his initiative as a student co-founder.
  - Distinguish real venture experience from speculative biotechnology ambitions.
- **Needs**:
  - A research-led presentation with the venture as a complementary strength.
  - Explicit, truthful descriptions of prototypes, concepts, and business activity.
- **Relevant stories**: BK-US-01, BK-US-03, BK-US-05.

## Persona 3: Portfolio Author

- **Name**: Nguyễn Lê Khôi.
- **Role**: The student whose portfolio is being customized and who may update it later.
- **Goals**:
  - Present a coherent account of his biology-focused education, research exposure, projects, entrepreneurship, and activities.
  - Replace or update content without editing presentation-heavy components.
  - Avoid publishing blank, fabricated, or someone else's contact details.
- **Needs**:
  - Typed, clearly named content and asset modules.
  - A single intentional presentation and predictable static deployment.
  - A visible indication that contact details are not yet available.
- **Relevant stories**: BK-US-05, BK-US-06, BK-US-07.
