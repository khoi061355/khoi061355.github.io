# Bioengineering Portfolio User Story Plan

## Purpose

Translate the approved portfolio requirements into visitor and author goals before implementation. The stories focus on trustworthy review of Khôi's student research, entrepreneurship, and supporting evidence.

## Proposed Personas

- **Research and admissions reviewer**: A teacher, university reviewer, or research mentor who needs to understand Khôi's academic direction and verify what he has actually done.
- **Biotech and entrepreneurship collaborator**: A visitor looking for a concise view of Khôi's interests in microbiology, environmental monitoring, and early-stage venture work.
- **Portfolio author**: Khôi, who needs an accurate, maintainable site that can be updated as his experience grows.

## Proposed Story Set

1. **Understand Khôi's bioengineering direction**: As a research or admissions reviewer, I want the opening to explain his biology focus and engineering interests so that I can quickly understand what motivates him.
2. **Review evidence-based research and projects**: As a reviewer, I want clear summaries of the microbiology internship, diagnostic and water-quality work, and selected projects so that I can assess his experience without confusing student work with professional or clinical claims.
3. **See an entrepreneurial approach to real problems**: As a biotech collaborator, I want to understand how Khôi connects scientific interests with practical solutions and business initiative so that I can see the potential of his interdisciplinary approach.
4. **Explore the portfolio on any device**: As a visitor, I want accessible navigation, readable content, and responsive imagery so that I can review the site comfortably on desktop or mobile.
5. **Know how to make contact without misleading details**: As a visitor, I want an honest contact status so that I understand public contact details are not yet available.
6. **Maintain an evolving student portfolio**: As Khôi, I want personal content and evidence to stay organized in the existing typed data and asset structure so that I can update the site without rewriting its presentation.

## Planning Decisions and Open Question

- The user approved one bioengineering-editorial presentation and removal of the Business implementation and selector support.
- **Research-led opening**: The user chose to lead with microbiology, diagnostics, and water-quality work, with entrepreneurship as a complementary strength.

- No additional runtime capability, user role, or backend workflow is in scope.
